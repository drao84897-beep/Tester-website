      How WebVerify AI Works

      A multi-vector inspection pipeline designed to assess digital authenticity without biased assumptions.

      1. Technical & SSRF Sandbox

      Every target URL undergoes strict SSRF validation before establishing connections. Our engine analyzes SSL/TLS certificates, HTTPS enforcement, and server headers.

      2. Deep Content & DOM Analysis

      The engine safely shallow-crawls key pages (About, Contact, Terms, Services) looking for real business identity markers vs dummy Lorem Ipsum templates and unfinished sections.

      3. Transparent Scoring & AI

      Signals are normalized into an explainable 0–100 score. Our modular AI layer synthesizes actionable strengths, risk warnings, and balanced due-diligence recommendations.

      Transparent Authenticity Scoring

      Unlike arbitrary URL blocklists, WebVerify AI calculates a transparent, weighted score ranging from 0 to 100. Every point added or deducted is tied directly to an explainable observation.

      75–100 LIKELY REAL BUSINESS — Coherent operational signals present
      45–74 NEEDS VERIFICATION — Incomplete identity or new domain
      0–44 LIKELY DEMO / DUMMY — Unfinished template or placeholder site
